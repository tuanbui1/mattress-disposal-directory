How to dispose of a mattress in Somerville, MA

Last checked Oct 2026. Every fact below links to an official source; confirm before acting.

Curbside pickup & scheduling

  • Eligible: households in buildings with 6 or fewer units receiving City trash collection; larger buildings with private service should ask their building manager[1].
  • Buy a Disposal Sticker first, then contact 311 (617-666-3311 or somervillema.gov/311) no later than 3:30 p.m. the day before collection[1].
  • Pickup is one day after your regular trash day (Friday trash households: Friday). Put items out from 4 p.m. the day before, by 7 a.m. pickup day, leaving a 36-inch clear sidewalk path[1].
  • Unscheduled mattresses put out with trash will be left and may be fined[1].
  • Furniture: City picks up two pieces per week with trash[3].

Mattress bag requirements

If soiled or bed-bug infested, call 311 for special instructions before buying a sticker[1]. Bed-bug infested items must be wrapped in plastic and clearly marked[3].

Fees & limits

  • $55 Disposal Sticker per item (mattress and box spring each need one), since Feb 1, 2026[1][2].
  • Buy online (somervillema.gov/payonline; mail can take up to 10 days), at DPW, One Franey Road (no cash), or City Clerk, 93 Highland Ave[1].
  • Limit: two bulk items (including mattresses/box springs) per week[1].

Massachusetts disposal ban

Massachusetts bans mattresses (including box springs and foundations) and textiles from disposal or transport for disposal, effective November 1, 2022[4]. Mattresses contaminated with mold, bodily fluids, insects, oil, or hazardous substances are excluded from the ban's definition[5]. Textiles covered include clothing, footwear, bedding, towels, curtains and fabric — even worn or stained items can go to textile recovery[5][6].

Drop-off & recycling

No city mattress drop-off site was found on the city pages checked.

Bye Bye Mattress: the Mattress Recycling Council's Bye Bye Mattress program currently runs only in California, Connecticut, Oregon and Rhode Island[7], so it does not operate in this state.
