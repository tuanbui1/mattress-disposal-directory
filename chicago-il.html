How to dispose of a mattress in Chicago, IL

Last checked Oct 2026. Every fact below links to an official source; confirm before acting.

Curbside pickup & scheduling

City crews collect from single-family homes and buildings of four units or less; others must arrange private collection[1][2]. The city lists a "Non-Scheduled Garbage" service[1]; request city services through 311 / CHI311[3].
