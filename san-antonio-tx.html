Bulky Item Disposal Guide

How to dispose of a mattress in San Antonio, TX

Last checked Oct 2026. Every fact below links to an official source; confirm before acting.

Curbside bulky collection

  • Mattresses are on the accepted list for San Antonio's curbside bulky item collection[1][5].
  • The City provides two free collections of brush and bulky items per year for each resident; brush and bulky items can't be mixed[6].
  • You'll get a door hanger 7 to 10 days before your collection week. The date on it is when to set your pile out, not the pickup day; collection can take up to a week. Use the City's Find My Collection Day tool for an estimated timeframe, or call 3-1-1 / 210-207-6000 if you didn't get a door hanger[1].
  • Have the pile out no later than 7 a.m. on the Monday of your collection week, within eight feet of the curb in front of your house, even if your garbage is collected in the alley[1].
  • Not in front of parked cars, under low-hanging limbs, on water meters, or on vacant lots[1]. Crews pass through an area only once[5].
  • Report a missed collection online or through 3-1-1[1].

Fees & limits

  • The two scheduled curbside collections are free[6]. Piles can be up to eight cubic yards[1].
  • Need it gone sooner? Request an out-of-cycle bulky collection online or via 3-1-1. Fees: $50 for one item or up to 4 cubic yards, plus $25 for each additional four cubic yards (or portion)[2]. Staff give a free estimate, and pickup happens within two working days after you pay[1].
  • Not accepted at the curb: brush and yard waste, concrete, construction or demolition material, glass and mirrors, household hazardous waste, roofing, soil and rocks[1].
  • A pile that contains brush, is obstructed, or is set out after crews have passed can bring a fine of up to $2,000 per day plus a collection and disposal fee[1][5].

Drop-off at a Bulky Waste Collection Center

  • Four City centers accept mattresses from residential customers[3].
  • Bring a recent CPS Energy bill showing payment of the City's environmental fee and a photo ID; if you pay that fee, your visit is free[3].
  • Not accepted: mattresses with blood stains or bed bugs, bed linens, clothes, bagged garbage, and loads from commercial haulers[3].
  • Limits: 4 cubic yards of bulky items and 1 cubic yard of construction material; truck loads must not be piled above the wheel fender[3].
  • Locations: Bitters, 1800 Wurzbach Parkway, 78216 (enter from Jones Maltsberger and Wurzbach Parkway); Frio City Road, 1531 Frio City Road, 78226; Culebra, 7030 Culebra Road, 78238; Rigsby Road, 2755 Rigsby Road, 78222[3].
  • Hours: Tuesday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.[3] (an older City services guide lists Saturday until noon and closed holidays[5], so call 3-1-1 before a late Saturday trip).

Free Landfill Day

  • Next date: Saturday, November 14, 2026, 8 a.m. to 1 p.m. Mattresses are accepted[4].
  • For City solid waste fee payers: bring a valid photo ID and your most recent CPS Energy statement showing the solid waste fee, and cover your load with a tarp[4].
  • Locations: Republic Services Landfill, 7000 IH 10 E, 78219; Waste Management Landfill, 8611 Covel Road, 78252 (a safety vest may be required)[4].

Illegal dumping

  • Putting furniture, appliances or other items at the curb outside a City-scheduled collection violates City code, and items may only go out after you receive a collection notice[6]. Out-of-cycle bulky items can get an administrative citation without prior notice[6].
  • Report dumping inside city limits to 3-1-1 / 210-207-6000 or with the 311 mobile app; outside city limits in Bexar County, call 210-335-6700[7].

Recycling & retailer take-back
