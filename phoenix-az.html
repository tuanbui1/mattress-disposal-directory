Bulky Item Disposal Guide

How to dispose of a mattress in Phoenix, AZ

Last checked Oct 2026. Every fact below links to an official source; confirm before acting.

Bulk trash pickup by appointment

  • Phoenix collects bulk trash by appointment only; the old quarterly schedule ended and there is no quarterly schedule from 2025 on[1][2].
  • Schedule with the online tool on the City's Bulk Trash page or call 602-262-6251. Most areas have appointments within a couple of weeks, and you can book up to a year ahead[1].
  • Each customer can schedule up to four bulk trash collections per calendar year, booked at least 3 business days in advance[2].
  • Cancel no later than two business days before the appointment (link in your confirmation email, or call 602-262-6251); timely cancellations don't count against your four[1].
  • Check first whether your HOA or neighborhood already has a community-wide appointment[1].
  • Holiday blackouts: no appointments on Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas or New Year's Day, or the 7 days after each[1].

Are mattresses accepted?

The City's Bulk Trash page says furniture is often considered bulk trash but does not name mattresses[1]. The City's bulk trash map service description lists mattresses among items often considered bulk trash[3]. An ordinance amending City Code Sec. 27-22 excludes mattresses or furniture known or suspected to be infested with bed bugs[4], but the current online City Code lists "all mattresses or furniture" as not acceptable for normal residential collection[5]. Because the official sources disagree, call 602-262-6251 to confirm before setting a mattress out, and never put out a mattress with bed bugs.

How to set it out

  • Place items no sooner than 7 days before your appointment[2][5] and by 5:30 a.m. on the appointment date (collection starts at 6 a.m.)[1][2].
  • One pile per household, curbside on the edge of your property parallel to the street, no more than 5 feet from the curb or front edge of the property, and at least 5 feet from any fixed object. Not on top of water meters, and not on the sidewalk or in the street[1][5].
  • Do not combine piles with neighbors; each household needs its own appointment[1].

Mattress bag requirements

The City pages checked do not mention a bagging requirement for mattresses. Not verified.

Fees & limits

  • Up to 10 cubic yards per appointment, roughly 17 ft x 4 ft x 4 ft[2][1].
  • Not accepted: household hazardous waste, tires, bricks, concrete, dirt and rocks, glass, loose yard waste, Bagster bags, more than 2 wooden pallets, and appliances that use refrigerants[1].
  • The City pages checked don't list a separate fee for a bulk trash appointment[1].

Drop-off at a City transfer station

  • City of Phoenix residential solid waste customers can drop off up to one ton each calendar month at no charge. Bring your City services (water) bill showing an active, current account and a photo ID with a matching name or address[6].
  • Above one ton, the gate rate is $55 per ton; other loads are $55 per ton with a $40 minimum. Cash and cards accepted; deposits are cash only[6].
  • Locations: North Gateway Transfer Station, 30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy., Phoenix 85085; 27th Avenue Transfer Station, 3060 S. 27th Ave., Phoenix 85009[6].
  • Hours: Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday and on listed holidays[6].
  • Loads must be secured; the unsecured load penalty is $20[6].
  • The transfer station page does not specifically mention mattresses; confirm at 602-262-6251[6].

Recycling & retailer take-back

Bye Bye Mattress: the Mattress Recycling Council's Bye Bye Mattress program currently runs only in California, Connecticut, Oregon and Rhode Island[7], so it does not operate in this state.

Book a paid hauler

If city service doesn't fit (too many items, large building, timing), a private junk-removal or mattress-recycling hauler is the fallback. Ask that they recycle the mattress.
