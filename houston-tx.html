How to dispose of a mattress in Houston, TX

Last checked Oct 2026. Every fact below links to an official source; confirm before acting.

Curbside heavy trash pickup

  • Mattresses and box springs are accepted in Houston's curbside Heavy Trash collection[1].
  • Heavy trash is collected every other month, in even-numbered months (February, April, June, August, October, December), for eligible addresses with City curbside service, up to six curbside collections per calendar year[1].
  • Each residence has an assigned collection day; look it up with the City's service lookup tool or the HTX App[1].
  • Set items out between 6:00 p.m. the Friday before and 7:00 a.m. on your collection day, just behind the curb line in front of your home, not in the street or on the sidewalk, and not under wires, signs or mailboxes or on top of meters or hydrants[1].
  • Eligibility: occupied residential units receiving City of Houston services; not available to multi-residential buildings with more than eight units[1].
  • For missed collections and other issues, contact 3-1-1 or submit a service request online; 311 service request status line: 832-394-1453 (Mon-Fri, 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.)[2].

Mattress bag requirements

The City heavy trash page checked does not mention a bagging requirement for mattresses. Not verified.

Fees & limits

  • Maximum 8 cubic yards per set-out (about the size of a minivan)[1].
  • Mixed piles: if authorized and unauthorized items are mixed, the whole pile may be refused[1].
  • Not accepted at the curb: everyday bagged garbage, electronics, paint and chemicals, dirt, roofing shingles, appliances with refrigerant unless tagged by a certified technician, and contractor debris[1].
  • The heavy trash page does not list a per-item fee for eligible residents[1].

Drop-off between heavy trash months

  • If you need to get rid of a mattress between heavy trash months or over the curbside limit, the City points residents to its Residential Drop-Off Centers[1], which it describes as a free service[3].
  • Locations: North, 9003 N Main 77022; Northwest, 14400 Sommermeyer 77041; Northeast, 5565 Kirkpatrick 77028; Southeast, 2240 Central Street 77017; South, 5100 Sunbeam 77033; Southwest, 10785 SW Freeway 77074[4].
  • Hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.; closed Sunday and Monday. Use is limited to four times per month (once per week); arrive at least 30 minutes before closing[4].
  • Mattresses are on the accepted bulk items list; furniture is limited to two large items[4].
  • Bring a current Texas-issued ID and a current City of Houston utility bill or lease with the same address[4].
  • Allowed vehicles: pickup trucks and small trailers up to 10 ft. Not allowed: U-Hauls, box trucks, Sprinter vans, contractor vehicles, dump trailers. You unload your own items[4].

Recycling & retailer take-back

Bye Bye Mattress: the Mattress Recycling Council's Bye Bye Mattress program currently runs only in California, Connecticut, Oregon and Rhode Island[5], so it does not operate in this state.

Book a paid hauler

If city service doesn't fit (too many items, large building, timing), a private junk-removal or mattress-recycling hauler is the fallback. Ask that they recycle the mattress.
