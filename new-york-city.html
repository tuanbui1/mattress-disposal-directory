Bulky Item Disposal Guide

How to dispose of a mattress in New York City, NY

Last checked Oct 2026. Every fact below links to an official source; confirm before acting.

Curbside pickup & scheduling

  • DSNY collects large items free at the curb; appointments are no longer offered or needed[1][2].
  • Mattresses and box springs go out on your trash-only day, not recycling day[3], curbside between 6 PM and midnight the night before[4].
  • Not in the street, in front of another property, blocking pedestrians, or on private property[2].

Mattress bag requirements

Must be fully enclosed in a securely sealed plastic bag to prevent bed bugs[5]. 311 says bags can be any color except red or orange and the City does not provide them; buy at department, home improvement, or moving supply stores[4]. (Note: DSNY's Collection Laws page says a clear plastic bag[5] — when in doubt use clear.)

Fees & limits

  • Collection is free; up to 6 bulk items per collection day[1].
  • Fines for improper mattress disposal: $50 / $100 / $200[5][4]; DSNY's item page says up to $300[3].

State disposal ban

No New York State mattress disposal ban was found in the official sources checked; not verified.

Drop-off & recycling

Bye Bye Mattress: the Mattress Recycling Council's Bye Bye Mattress program currently runs only in California, Connecticut, Oregon and Rhode Island[6], so it does not operate in this state.
