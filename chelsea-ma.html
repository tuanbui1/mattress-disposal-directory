How to dispose of a mattress in Chelsea, MA

Last checked Oct 2026. Every fact below links to an official source; confirm before acting.

Curbside pickup & scheduling

  • Mattress collection days are Monday and Thursday. Pay, get your collection sticker, then call 311 (617-466-4209) to schedule[1].
  • Leave the mattress outside by 7 a.m. on the scheduled day with the sticker attached; unscheduled mattresses may be ticketed. Tell 311 if it has bed bugs[1].
  • Mattresses and box springs are not accepted in regular trash; furniture is limited to 2 items per week with trash[3].

Mattress bag requirements

Mattress must be wrapped in plastic[1]; items not wrapped will not be collected and may get a citation[4].

Fees & limits

  • City trash customers: $25 per mattress/box spring (since Feb 1, 2026)[1][2].
  • Private-hauler customers: ask your hauler first; otherwise the City charges $40 per item[1].
  • Pay at City Hall, online ("Apply For A Permit" → "Curbside Mattress Pick Up"), or by check to 500 Broadway, Room 310[1].
  • Illegal dumping fines: $50 / $150 / $300[2].

Massachusetts disposal ban

Massachusetts bans mattresses (including box springs and foundations) and textiles from disposal or transport for disposal, effective November 1, 2022[5]. Mattresses contaminated with mold, bodily fluids, insects, oil, or hazardous substances are excluded from the ban's definition[6]. Textiles covered include clothing, footwear, bedding, towels, curtains and fabric — even worn or stained items can go to textile recovery[6][7].

Chelsea offers free curbside textile pickup on Sundays via 311 or helpsy.co/chelseama[8].

Drop-off & recycling

No city mattress drop-off site was found on the city pages checked.

Bye Bye Mattress: the Mattress Recycling Council's Bye Bye Mattress program currently runs only in California, Connecticut, Oregon and Rhode Island[9], so it does not operate in this state.
